**How the checker works:**
- It uses the fixed limits you gave: 24 fps, at most 20 characters a second, 42 characters a line and 2 lines, and 5/6 s to 7 s on screen.
- All comparisons use whole milliseconds with no rounding or tolerance. For example, characters × 1000 must not exceed 20 × duration, and gap × 24 must be at least 2000.
- Overlap counts as its own failure, not a gap failure. Gap and overlap failures go on the earlier cue.
- Zero-duration cues fail `duration` and skip the characters-a-second check.

**Edge cases I tested, all with the expected results:**
- An empty file gives zero cues and exit code 0.
- Exact limits behave as intended: 833 ms fails and 834 ms passes; a gap of 83 ms fails and 84 ms passes; 20 characters a second passes and 21 fails; 7000 ms passes and 7001 ms fails.
- Zero duration fails `duration` without dividing by zero, and an overlap is reported correctly.
- Formatting tags are removed and leading spaces kept; three lines fail `line_count`, and a 43-character line fails `line_length`.
- A byte-order mark with CRLF line endings, and cue numbers that skip, are read correctly.

**Choices I made beyond your spec:**
- **Bad input:** if the file is unreadable, isn't valid UTF-8, has a block without a valid `HH:MM:SS,mmm --> HH:MM:SS,mmm` timing line, or has a cue that ends before it starts, the checker prints an error to stderr and exits with code 2. It prints no JSON in that case, so CI still fails.
- **Blank lines inside a cue:** a block with no timing line that follows a cue is treated as more text for that cue. That covers cues containing a whitespace-only line.
