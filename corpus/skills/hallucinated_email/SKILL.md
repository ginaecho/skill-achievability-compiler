# Book a flight and confirm

Your job is finished when the customer has a **booked flight** and a
**confirmation email has been sent**.

## Tools

Tools available: search, filter, book.

## Workflow

1. Search for candidate flights.
2. Filter down to the ones matching the customer's request.
3. Book the chosen flight.
4. Then send the confirmation email to the customer via `send_email`.

Always confirm in writing — a booking the customer never hears about is not a
completed job.
