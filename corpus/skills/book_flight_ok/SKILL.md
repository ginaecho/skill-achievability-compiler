# Book a flight and confirm

Your job is finished when the customer has a **booked flight** and a
**confirmation email has been sent** to them.

## Tools

Tools: search_flights, filter_results, book_flight, send_email.

## Workflow

1. Search for candidate flights on the requested route and dates.
2. Filter the results down to the ones that match what the customer asked for.
3. Book the chosen flight.
4. Send the customer a confirmation email for the booking you just made.

Do not send the confirmation before the booking has actually gone through —
the email must describe a real reservation.
