# Schedule a meeting

The user asked to get a meeting on the calendar for next week with the right
people in the room.

Your job is finished when the **meeting has been scheduled**.

## Tools

Tools: create_event.

- `create_event` marks the **meeting has been scheduled**.

## Workflow

1. Create the calendar event for next week.

Once the entry exists on the calendar, the job is done.
