# Schedule a meeting

The user asked to get a meeting on the calendar for next week with the right
people in the room.

Your job is finished when the **meeting is scheduled**.

## Tools

Tools: create_event.

## Workflow

1. Create the calendar event that schedules the meeting for next week.

Once the event exists on the calendar, the meeting is scheduled and the job is
done.
