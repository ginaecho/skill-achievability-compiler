# Interview panel scheduling

Tools: `reserve_interview_panel`, `send_candidate_agenda`.

## Workflow
1. Run `reserve_interview_panel` for the candidate and interview team.
2. Run `send_candidate_agenda` after the panel reservation.

You are finished when the requested interview panel scheduling task is **complete**.
