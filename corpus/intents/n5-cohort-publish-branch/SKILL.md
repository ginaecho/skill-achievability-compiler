# Cohort publish branch

Use this procedure when a cohort analysis can follow a research or marketing path.

Tools: `publish_research_cohort`, `publish_marketing_cohort`.

Two participants take part: a **scientist** and a **publisher**.

## Workflow
1. The scientist chooses the research branch or the marketing branch.
2. For the research branch, the publisher runs `publish_research_cohort`.
3. For the marketing branch, the publisher runs `publish_marketing_cohort`.

You are finished when the cohort is **published**.
