# Research notice publication

Use this procedure when a research coordinator prepares an office notice for publication.

Tools: `draft_research_notice`, `publish_research_notice`.

## Workflow
1. Use `draft_research_notice` to draft the notice.
2. Use `publish_research_notice` to publish the notice.

You are finished when the notice is **notice_published**.
