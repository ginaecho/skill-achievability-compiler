# Campaign spend plan

Use this procedure for a marketer preparing a paid campaign plan with spend at or below 1000.

Tools: `pull_metrics`, `forecast_spend`, `submit_plan`.

## Workflow
1. Use `pull_metrics` to gather the campaign metrics.
2. Use `forecast_spend` to produce the spend forecast.
3. Use `submit_plan` to submit the finished plan.

You are finished when the plan is **submitted** and spend is **at or below 1000**.
