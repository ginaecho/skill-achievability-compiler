# Rights clearance story

Use this procedure for a publisher preparing a rights-cleared story for publication.

Tools: `draft_story`, `request_clearance`, `publish_story`.

## Workflow
1. Use `draft_story` to draft the story.
2. Use `request_clearance` to handle rights clearance.
3. Use `publish_story` to publish the story.

You are finished when rights are **cleared** and the story is **published**.
