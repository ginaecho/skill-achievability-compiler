# Contract publication

Use this procedure for a contract manager publishing a supplier contract record.

Tools: draft_supplier_contract, publish_contract_record.

## Workflow
1. Use `draft_supplier_contract` to draft the supplier contract.
2. Use `publish_contract_record` to publish the contract record.

You are finished when the contract record is **published**.
