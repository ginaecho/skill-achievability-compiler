# Schedule a meeting next week

Use this procedure when a meeting needs to be placed on the calendar with the right people included.

Tools: `create_event`.

## Workflow

1. Identify a suitable slot next week for the requested meeting.
2. Include the right attendees for the meeting topic.
3. Create the calendar entry with `create_event`.

You are finished when the meeting is **scheduled** for next week with the right attendees.
