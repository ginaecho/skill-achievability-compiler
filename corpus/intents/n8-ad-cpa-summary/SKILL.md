# Ad CPA summary

Use this procedure for an analyst preparing a paid media summary with CPA at or below 50.

Tools: `load_ad_data`, `optimize_bid`, `publish_summary`.

## Workflow
1. Use `load_ad_data` to load the advertising data.
2. Use `optimize_bid` to optimize bids.
3. Use `publish_summary` to publish the summary.

You are finished when the summary is **published** and CPA is **at or below 50**.
