# Syllabus review roster

Use this procedure when a program coordinator gathers a syllabus packet and creates a department reviewer participant for the review record.

Tools: `collect_syllabus_packet`, `record_department_review`.

Two participants take part: a **program_coordinator** and a **department_reviewer**.

## Workflow
1. The program coordinator uses `collect_syllabus_packet` to collect the packet.
2. Create the department_reviewer participant.
3. The department_reviewer uses `record_department_review` to record the review.

You are finished when the review is **review_recorded**.
