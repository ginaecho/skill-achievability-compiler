# Dispatch a pop-up courier

Use this procedure when a dispatcher brings a courier into a delivery run.

Tools: `issue_manifest`.

Two participants take part: a **dispatcher** and a courier added during the run.

## Workflow
1. Add a courier participant during dispatch.
2. The dispatcher runs `issue_manifest`.

You are finished when the manifest is **issued**.
