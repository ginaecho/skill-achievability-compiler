# Incident status closure

Use this procedure when an incident commander needs customer notification and incident closure.

Tools: `draft_update`, `send_status`, `close_incident`.

## Workflow
1. Use `draft_update` to prepare the incident update.
2. Use `send_status` after the update is drafted.
3. Use `close_incident` after status is sent.

You are finished when customers are **notified** and the incident is **closed**.
