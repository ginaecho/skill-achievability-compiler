# Supplier onboarding packet

Use this procedure for onboarding a supplier into procurement operations.

Tools: collect_supplier_profile, verify_tax_form, create_vendor_record, send_onboarding_packet.

## Workflow
1. Use `collect_supplier_profile` to collect the supplier profile.
2. Use `verify_tax_form` to verify the tax form.
3. Use `create_vendor_record` to create the vendor record.
4. Use `send_onboarding_packet` to send the onboarding packet.

You are finished when the vendor record is **created** and the onboarding packet is **sent**.
