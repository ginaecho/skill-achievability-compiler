# Social video scheduling

Use this procedure for a producer preparing a social video for publication.

Tools: `ingest_media`, `generate_caption`, `schedule_post`.

## Workflow
1. Use `ingest_media` to add the video asset.
2. Use `generate_caption` to prepare the caption.
3. Use `schedule_post` to schedule the post.

You are finished when the caption is **ready** and the post is **scheduled**.
