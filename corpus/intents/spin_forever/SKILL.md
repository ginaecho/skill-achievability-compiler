# Iterate on a report for publication

Use this procedure when the goal is a published report and the work proceeds through repeated drafting and review.

Tools: `draft`, `publish`.

## Workflow

1. Create or revise the report with `draft`.
2. Review the current report.
3. Continue the drafting and review cycle, iteration after iteration.

You are finished when the report is **published** through `publish`.
