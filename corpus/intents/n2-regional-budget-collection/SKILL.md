# Regional budget collection

Two participants take part: a **budget coordinator** and a **regional analyst**.

Tools: create_budget_workspace, collect_region_forecast, consolidate_budget_plan.

## Workflow
1. The budget coordinator uses `create_budget_workspace` to create the workspace.
2. Bring in a regional analyst for the collection run.
3. The regional analyst uses `collect_region_forecast` to collect the forecast.
4. The budget coordinator uses `consolidate_budget_plan` to consolidate the budget plan.

You are finished when the budget plan is **consolidated**.
