# Policy update release

Use this procedure for counsel releasing an updated company policy.

Tools: `draft_policy`, `publish_policy`.

## Workflow
1. Use `draft_policy` to draft the policy update.
2. Use `publish_policy` to publish the drafted policy update.

You are finished when the policy update is **published**.
