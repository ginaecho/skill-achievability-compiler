# Vendor compliance archive

Use this procedure for an analyst completing a vendor compliance evidence record.

Tools: `collect_attestation`, `review_policy`, `archive_record`.

## Workflow
1. Use `collect_attestation` to gather the vendor attestation.
2. Use `review_policy` to complete the policy review.
3. Use `archive_record` to archive the evidence record.

You are finished when the policy is **reviewed** and the record is **archived**.
