# Timecard audit sampling

Tools: `sample_timecards`, `publish_audit_summary`.

## Workflow
1. Run `sample_timecards` to prepare the audit sample.
2. Run `publish_audit_summary` after the sample is prepared.
3. Complete with a published summary and a sample count from 10 through 20.

You are finished when the requested timecard audit sampling task is **complete**.
