# Employee onboarding

Use this procedure to complete the core account and badge steps for a new employee.

Tools: create_account, issue_badge.

## Workflow
1. Use `create_account` to create the employee account.
2. Use `issue_badge` to issue the employee badge after the account is created.

You are finished when the employee account is **created** and the badge is **issued**.
