# Claim account settlement

Use this procedure when a billing specialist must settle an administrative claim account.

Tools: `review_claim_account`, `apply_payment`.

## Workflow
1. Use `review_claim_account` to review the account.
2. Use `apply_payment` to settle the account.

You are finished when the claim account is **account_settled**.
