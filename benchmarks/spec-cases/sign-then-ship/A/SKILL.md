# Sign Then Ship

Your job is finished when the artifact is **signed** and **shipped**.

## Tools

Tools: sign, ship.

Each tool is a script: `python tools/<tool>.py`.

## Workflow

1. Sign it with `sign`.
2. Ship it with `ship`.
